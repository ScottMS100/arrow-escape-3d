Arrow Escape 3D

Tap an arrow to slide it out the way it points. If another arrow is in its path it bumps and you lose a heart. Clear the board.

Inside: a see-through cube of 3D arrows. Outside: a solid shape (cube, steps, castle and more, a new one each level) with flat arrows on its surface. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap an arrow to launch it.

How to play

Every arrow can slide out of the board in the direction it points. Clear the whole board to win the level.

The rules

  • Tap an arrow to send it. It slides along the way its head points and off the board.
  • If another arrow is in its path it bumps and you lose a heart. You have 3 hearts per level; run out and you retry the level.
  • So work out which arrows have a clear path first, and clear those to open up the rest.
  • Levels are always solvable, and they get bigger and harder as you go. Your progress is saved on this device.

Buttons

  • 💡 Hint highlights an arrow that can leave right now.
  • ↻ Restart starts the level over with full hearts.
  • ☰ Menu opens this menu. Use Resume to go back to your game.

The three modes

  • 2D Classic: a flat board packed with winding arrows. Zoom in to tap small arrows.
  • 3D Cube Inside: a see-through block of 3D arrows. Rotate it to see arrows hiding behind others.
  • 3D Shapes Outside: a solid shape (a new one each level) with flat arrows on its surface. Arrows can bend over edges and slide off the edge they point at.

Moving around

  • Rotate (3D modes): drag with a finger or the mouse, in any direction.
  • Zoom: pinch on a phone, or use the scroll wheel on a computer.
  • Double-tap to launch (menu option): the first tap highlights an arrow and a second tap within 4 seconds sends it. This stops accidental launches on a phone.
